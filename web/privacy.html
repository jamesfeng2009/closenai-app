隐私政策

生效日期：2026-11-01　最后更新：2026-10-21

本政策说明 ［运营主体全称］（下称「我们」）在你使用 ClosenAI 移动应用及相关服务时，如何收集、使用、存储和保护你的信息。

This policy explains how [Legal entity name] ("we") collects, uses, stores and protects your information when you use the ClosenAI mobile application and related services.

一句话版本：你写下的内容是你自己的。我们不做广告，不把你的内容卖给任何人，也不用你的内容训练公开的 AI 模型。你可以随时导出全部数据，或永久删除账号。

In one sentence: what you write belongs to you. We do not run ads, do not sell your content, and do not use it to train public AI models. You can export everything or permanently delete your account at any time.

1. 我们收集的信息

类别具体内容为什么需要
账号信息 邮箱地址；如你选择「通过 Apple 登录」，则为 Apple 提供的匿名转发地址与用户标识 创建账号、登录、找回
你主动写下的内容 反思记录、对话内容、心情记录、目标、行动、结果、你上传的文字 这是服务的核心，用于生成回应与跨时间回顾
设备与推送 推送令牌、设备型号、操作系统版本、应用版本、时区 发送你设定的提醒；保证功能兼容
订阅状态 是否有效订阅、订阅档位、到期时间（由 Apple 提供，我们拿不到你的支付卡信息） 解锁对应功能
使用记录 功能使用次数、页面访问、错误日志、崩溃信息 修复缺陷、判断功能是否有人用

我们不收集：通讯录、短信、通话记录、相册（除非你主动选择图片）、精确位置、其他应用的使用情况。

2. 我们如何使用这些信息

  • 提供核心功能：记录、AI 回应、跨时间模式回顾、你设定的到期回访。
  • 发送你主动开启的提醒与回访通知。你可以随时在系统设置或应用内关闭。
  • 保障安全：识别滥用、防止刷量、排查故障。
  • 改进产品：以聚合方式统计功能使用情况。

我们不会：向你投放基于内容的广告；把你的内容出售、出租或交换给第三方；用你的内容训练面向公众开放的 AI 模型。

3. AI 处理

ClosenAI 的回应与洞察由大语言模型生成。为了生成回应，你本次输入的相关内容会被发送至我们委托的模型服务商处理。

  • 我们会向服务商发送生成回应所需的最小内容，不发送你的账号标识以外的身份信息。
  • 我们与服务商的协议要求其不将你的内容用于训练其模型。
  • AI 输出可能不准确、不完整或不符合你的实际情况。它不构成医疗、心理、法律或财务建议。

AI responses and insights are generated by large language models. The relevant parts of what you enter are sent to our contracted model providers solely to generate a response. Our agreements with them prohibit using your content for model training. AI output may be inaccurate and is not medical, psychological, legal or financial advice.

4. 第三方处理者

我们使用以下类别的服务商处理数据，它们仅能按我们的指示处理，且不得用于自身目的：

  • 云托管与数据库（存储你的账号与内容）
  • 大语言模型服务商（生成回应）
  • 推送服务（投递通知）
  • 产品分析（聚合统计使用情况）
  • 应用商店与订阅管理（Apple）

具体服务商名单可向我们索取。

5. 存储位置与跨境传输

我们的服务器位于 ［部署区域，例如：美国 / 新加坡］。如果你位于其他司法辖区，你的信息会被传输至该区域并在那里处理。我们会采取合同与技术措施保护传输中的数据。

Our servers are located in [region]. If you are located elsewhere, your information will be transferred to and processed in that region, with contractual and technical safeguards.

6. 保留期限

  • 账号存续期间，我们保留你的内容以提供服务。
  • 你删除某条记录后，它在 30 天内从生产系统中移除（备份中的副本会在备份轮换周期内过期）。
  • 你注销账号后，账号进入 30 天冷静期；期间可撤销。期满后，你的内容、记忆模型与关联记录被永久删除。
  • 法律要求保留的记录（如订阅凭证）按法定期限保留。

7. 你的权利

无论你所在辖区是否有强制规定，我们为你提供以下能力，且都在应用内直接可用：

权利如何行使

删除单条内容在对应条目上删除

撤回同意设置 → 隐私与安全，关闭对应授权（如推送、分析）
反对处理 / 投诉发信至下方邮箱，我们会在 30 天内答复

8. 儿童与未成年人

本服务不面向 13 岁以下儿童。若你未满所在辖区的成年年龄，请在监护人同意下使用。若我们发现在未获同意的情况下收集了儿童信息，会尽快删除。

9. 安全

传输过程使用 TLS 加密，存储数据加密保存。访问生产数据需要授权并留有审计记录。但没有任何系统能保证绝对安全，请妥善保管你的账号凭据。

10. 非医疗服务声明

ClosenAI 不是医疗服务，不提供诊断、治疗或危机干预。它不能替代精神科医生、心理治疗师或心理咨询师。如果你处于危机中或有伤害自己的想法，请立即联系当地急救电话或危机热线。

ClosenAI is not a medical service and does not provide diagnosis, treatment or crisis intervention. It is not a substitute for a psychiatrist, therapist or counselor. If you are in crisis or having thoughts of self-harm, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line immediately.

11. 政策变更

我们更新本政策时会在应用内提示，重大变更会提前通知。继续使用即表示你接受更新后的政策。

12. 联系我们

隐私问题、数据请求或投诉：support@closenai.app

我们通常在 30 天内答复。

Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-01　Last updated: 2026-10-21

This policy explains how [Legal entity name] ("we") collects, uses, stores and protects your information when you use the ClosenAI mobile application and related services.

1. Information we collect

  • Account: email address; or, if you use Sign in with Apple, the private relay address and user identifier Apple provides.
  • Content you write: reflections, conversations, mood entries, goals, actions, outcomes, and any text you upload. This is the core of the service.
  • Device and push: push token, device model, OS version, app version, time zone — to deliver reminders you set and to keep the app working.
  • Subscription status: whether a subscription is active, which tier, and expiry (provided by Apple; we never see your payment card).
  • Usage data: feature usage counts, screen views, error and crash logs.

We do not collect: contacts, SMS, call logs, your photo library (unless you pick an image), precise location, or other apps' usage.

2. How we use it

  • To provide core features: recording, AI responses, cross-time pattern reviews, and the follow-ups you schedule.
  • To send notifications you have enabled. You can turn these off at any time.
  • To keep the service safe: abuse detection, rate limiting, debugging.
  • To improve the product using aggregated usage statistics.

We do not run content-based ads, sell or rent your content, or use it to train publicly available AI models.

3. AI processing

Responses and insights are generated by large language models. The relevant parts of what you enter are sent to our contracted model providers solely to generate a response. Our agreements prohibit them from using your content to train their models. AI output may be inaccurate or incomplete and is not medical, psychological, legal or financial advice.

4. Processors

We use providers for cloud hosting and database, large language models, push delivery, product analytics, and app store / subscription management. They process data only on our instructions. A current list is available on request.

5. Storage and international transfers

Our servers are located in [region]. If you are elsewhere, your information is transferred to and processed in that region under contractual and technical safeguards.

6. Retention

  • While your account exists, we retain your content to provide the service.
  • Deleted entries are removed from production within 30 days; backup copies expire with the backup rotation cycle.
  • After you delete your account, a 30-day cooling-off period applies (reversible). After that, your content, memory model and linked records are permanently deleted.
  • Records required by law (e.g. subscription receipts) are kept for the statutory period.

7. Your rights

Regardless of your jurisdiction, these are available directly in the app: access and export (Settings → Data → Export), correction, deletion of individual entries, account deletion (Settings → Privacy & Security → Delete Account), and withdrawal of consent. For objections or complaints, email us; we respond within 30 days.

8. Children

The service is not directed to children under 13. If you are below the age of majority where you live, use it with guardian consent. If we learn we collected a child's information without consent, we will delete it.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS and encrypted at rest. Production access requires authorization and is audited. No system is perfectly secure; please protect your credentials.

10. Not a medical service

ClosenAI is not a medical service and does not provide diagnosis, treatment or crisis intervention. It is not a substitute for a psychiatrist, therapist or counselor. If you are in crisis or having thoughts of self-harm, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line immediately.

11. Changes

We will notify you in the app when this policy changes, and in advance for material changes. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.
